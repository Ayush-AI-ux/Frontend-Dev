Top 3 Travel Destinations

Curated by Ayush Mittal

1. Paris, France

Paris, the city of lights, is famous for art, culture, and iconic architecture. Explore the Eiffel Tower, the Louvre, and charming cafés along the Seine.

“A magical city that feels like stepping into a painting — every street tells a story.”

2. Kyoto, Japan

Kyoto blends ancient temples, serene gardens, and traditional tea houses. It offers a peaceful dive into Japan’s cultural heritage.

“A serene paradise — temples and cherry blossoms make Kyoto truly unforgettable.”

3. Santorini, Greece

Famous for whitewashed houses and blue-domed churches, Santorini offers stunning sunsets and crystal-clear waters — perfect for a relaxing escape.

“The cliffside view during sunset is mesmerizing — pure bliss.”

Travel Tips

  • Pack light and carry essentials.
  • Learn basic local phrases for communication.
  • Keep digital copies of important documents.
  • Respect local customs and traditions.
  • Stay hydrated and plan your schedule wisely.
